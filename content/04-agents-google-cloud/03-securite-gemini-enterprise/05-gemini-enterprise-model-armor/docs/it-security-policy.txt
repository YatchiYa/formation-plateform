IT security policy (demo document for the tutorial)

Never share your password, MFA codes or API keys, including with colleagues or IT support.
Do not paste customer personal data or secrets into AI tools other than the approved
Gemini Enterprise app. Company AI tools are protected by Model Armor, which blocks prompt
injection, jailbreak attempts, malicious links and sensitive data such as card numbers.
Report suspicious emails to security@example.com. Laptops must be locked when unattended.
